1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This proposal presents a comprehensive Engineering Materials Testing Management System (EMTMS) designed to digitize laboratory operations and automate engineering calculations for construction materials testing. The proposed solution will replace manual processes with an integrated, web-based platform that improves accuracy, efficiency, and reporting quality for geotechnical, concrete, and rock testing laboratories.

The system will standardize calculations according to international testing standards (BS, ASTM, AASHTO, ISRM), reduce manual errors, and generate professional test reports with automated certificates. It addresses the growing need for digital transformation in materials testing laboratories while maintaining compliance with industry standards and accreditation requirements.

==================================================

2. PROJECT OBJECTIVES

The primary objectives of this project are to:

• Automate engineering materials testing processes and calculations
• Standardize calculations according to applicable testing standards (BS 1377, BS EN 12390, ASTM D3167, etc.)
• Reduce manual calculations and reporting errors
• Improve sample tracking and laboratory efficiency
• Centralize test records and historical results
• Generate professional, printable test certificates and reports
• Implement role-based access control and audit trails
• Provide real-time data analysis and reporting
• Ensure data integrity and backup/recovery capabilities

3. PROPOSED SYSTEM MODULES

A. SOIL TESTING MODULE
- Moisture content determination from BS 1377
- Particle size distribution / sieve analysis per BS 1377-2:1990
- Atterberg limits (liquid and plastic limits) per BS 1377-2:1990
- Proctor compaction tests per BS 1377-4:1990
- California Bearing Ratio (CBR) per BS 1377-4:1990 / ASTM D1883
- Field density tests per BS 1377-4:1990
- Specific gravity determination
- Soil classification (BS 1377 / ASTM D2487 / AASHTO M 145)
- Hydrometer analysis for fine-grained soils
- Granular soil classification per BS 1377-2:1990
- Ripening and consolidation analysis
- Swelling potential tests

B. CONCRETE TESTING MODULE
- Concrete cube and cylinder compressive strength per BS EN 12390-3:2002
- Slump tests per BS 1881-109:1995
- Concrete density and specific gravity per BS EN 12390-7:2009
- Aggregate grading per BS 1198-1:1994
- Aggregate crushing and impact values per BS 812-110:1990
- Water absorption per BS EN 1097-6:1997
- Flexural strength tests per BS EN 12390-18:2010
- Air content and properties testing
- Setting time tests per BS 1881-110:1995
- Freezing and thawing resistance
- Elastic modulus determination
- Creep and shrinkage analysis

C. ROCK TESTING MODULE
- Uniaxial compressive strength per ISRM Suggested Method (ASTM D5731)
- Point load strength index per ISRM / ASTM D5731
- Brazilian tensile strength per ISRM / ASTM D3967
- Rock density and specific gravity per ISRM / ASTM C127
- Water absorption and porosity per ISRM / ASTM D2328
- Durability and soundness testing per ISRM / ASTM C170
- Indirect shear strength tests
- Triaxial compression tests
- Direct tension tests
- Tunnel face stability analysis
